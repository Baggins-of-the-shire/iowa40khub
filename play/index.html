Play Now

Find someone near you to play a game of 40k. Opening soon

Get started

  1. Create your account. Enter your email and tap the link we send you. No password to remember.

    Create an account Already have one? Sign in

  2. Set up your profile. Pick your faction and how many points you play.
  3. Turn on Ready now when you're free. You show up on a live list of players who want a game.
  4. Challenge someone. Pick a shop you can both play at and a time. Once they accept, you get quick messages like "Running late" or "I'm here".
  5. Play, then tap Game finished. It adds to the count below.

Steps 1 and 2 work now. The rest opens soon. No phone numbers or Discord names are ever shared.

Playing safe

  • Players must be 18 or over.
  • Games happen at shops, never at someone's home or a private place.
  • The hub doesn't check who players are. Use your own judgment, and leave if something feels off.
  • Keep your phone number and address to yourself until you know someone.
  • In danger right now? Call 911 and tell the shop staff.

The hub is a free fan project to help players find games and build the community. It isn't responsible for what happens when you meet, so please be respectful and play smart.

Report or block a player

Someone didn't show up, was rude or threatening, or seems under 18? Tell Josh privately. Include their username and what happened. They aren't told who reported them.

Email a report to bbagginsoftheshire3791@gmail.com

When Play Now opens, each match card gets a Report or block button with the username filled in. Blocking hides you from each other on the list and stops them challenging you. They aren't told.
