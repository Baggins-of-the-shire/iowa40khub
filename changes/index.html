What changed

Updates to the hub, newest first.

  1. Wednesday, October 14, 2026

    Space Marines is now the default look (you can still pick Adeptus Mechanicus or any other theme on your account page). Each faction also has its own original pixel-art character.

  2. Wednesday, October 14, 2026

    Events now shows three Sunday games around the metro, each marked 'not confirmed yet' until the shop confirms the time.

  3. Wednesday, October 14, 2026

    Online groups have their own Local Groups page. A Play Now button in the header shows how matchmaking will work, and your account lives there. The New to 40k guide now links Games Workshop's free Battle Report.

  4. Wednesday, October 14, 2026

    Where to play now lists seven shops in the Des Moines metro and two online groups where local players set up games.

  5. Tuesday, October 13, 2026

    First version of the hub is live: a New to 40k guide, Where to play and Events pages (filling up as shops say yes), a feedback form, and a poster page with a QR code.
